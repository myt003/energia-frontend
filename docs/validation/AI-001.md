## Grounded task explanation

1. Open Energia and wait for **Synchronized with Energia server**.
2. Open any compliance check or energy habit card.
3. Confirm the dedicated task detail screen contains:
   - What to do
   - Why assigned/selected
   - Evidence requirement and pass condition
   - Safety summary
   - Recommendation owner for habits
   - Published rule/template ID and immutable version
   - Approved source title/version/section
4. When the model succeeds, confirm the badge says **AI simplified**.
5. The AI wording must not add measurements, permissions, diagnoses, or safety
   actions that are absent from the structured task.
6. If the provider is unavailable, confirm the screen uses **Structured
   fallback** rather than hiding the task or inventing text.

## Photo evidence verification

1. Open a routine energy habit.
2. Confirm **Add photo or video evidence** is always visible in the fixed bottom
   action bar; no scrolling is required to find it.
3. Tap it → **Take photo** or choose an existing
   photo.
4. Capture a clear full machine/display image and submit it.
5. Confirm the visible synchronization states:
   - Evidence uploaded
   - AI verification processing
   - Verified, Potential problem, Insufficient evidence, or Human review required
6. Open Django Admin → **Evidence**, **AI jobs**, **Verification results**, and
   **Model request logs**.
7. Confirm checksum, task, employee, deterministic image checks, model, token
   use, latency, result, confidence, and human-review flag are stored.
8. A routine photo may auto-verify only when quality is adequate, consistency is
   positive, there are no findings, confidence is at least 0.90, and the task is
   neither urgent nor event-triggered.

## Simple classroom test

1. Today → **Energy habits**.
2. Open **Classroom demo: photograph an unoccupied room’s lighting state**.
   This validation-only task is sorted before the other habits.
3. Read the privacy and safety instruction.
4. Use the fixed **Add photo or video evidence** button.
5. Take one clear photo that shows:
   - An unoccupied classroom
   - The visible ceiling-light state
   - No faces, names, student work, screens, or personal information
6. Stay on the floor and do not touch wiring, electrical panels, or protected
   controls.
7. Submit and wait for the result. Depending on framing, the conservative model
   may return Verified, Insufficient evidence, or Human review required.

## Guided machine video

1. Open a machine-specific task.
2. Tap **Add photo or video evidence** → **Record guided video**.
3. Confirm the app shows the assigned machine and these prompts before camera:
   - Full accessible machine first
   - Slow movement toward display/condition points
   - Useful sound may be retained but is not used as an AI diagnosis
   - Never open panels, reset, repair, or bypass guards
   - Maximum 60 seconds
4. Record and upload the clip.
5. Expected backend behavior:
   - FFprobe validates duration
   - FFmpeg samples at most five ordered JPEG frames
   - Only sampled images and task context reach the vision model
   - Cited frame timestamps are returned when supplied by the model
   - Video verification always requires authorized human review
6. Confirm no native-video or audio-transcription claim is shown.

## Conservative-result checks

- A blurry/small image should produce **Insufficient evidence**.
- Visible inconsistency or possible issue should produce **Potential problem**.
- Provider failure must preserve evidence and produce **Human review required**.
- Machine findings must be called potential issues, never confirmed engineering
  diagnoses.
- EXIF, AI confidence, or a generic video must never be treated as proof of an
  internal fault, temperature, leak, completed repair, or safety certification.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Task explanations are server-synchronized and citation-grounded
- [ ] AI output cannot replace structured instruction/pass/evidence fields
- [ ] Deterministic fallback works without a model
- [ ] Photo and video capture are linked to the task and employee
- [ ] Evidence uploads are idempotent and checksum-addressed
- [ ] AI processing uses a database-backed worker rather than the mobile request
- [ ] Model calls store model/request ID, token usage, latency, and request hash
- [ ] Raw prompts/media are not copied into model-request logs
- [ ] Photo auto-verification obeys deterministic low-risk thresholds
- [ ] Guided videos use sampled ordered frames and always require human review
- [ ] Status, summary, confidence, findings, and cited frames appear in mobile
- [ ] Admin can inspect evidence, queue jobs, results, frames, and usage logs
